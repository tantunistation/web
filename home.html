The Anatomy of Mersin Tantuni

The Tantuni Ritual

Not ordinary shawarma, doner, or kebab. Tantuni is an UNESCO-recognized Turkish culinary heritage preparation built around intense heat, rhythm, speed, and precision.

1. The Searing Sac

Finely diced prime meat is first simmered, then transferred to the depression of a blistering hot copper sac pan. Light water splashes create plumes of steam that tenderize the meat in seconds.

2. Steamed Lavash

Thin artisan lavash is pressed directly against the sizzling meat and spices in the pan, absorbing warm vapor, aromatics, and rich savory juices before rolling.

3. Sumac & Pickled Urfa

Finely sliced red onions rubbed by hand with tangy sumac, crisp parsley, and ripe summer tomatoes provide the vital cooling crunch against the hot meat.

4. Foamy Ayran & Şalgam

Pair your roll with ice-cold churned foam Ayran or spicy fermented purple turnip juice (şalgam suyu)—the exact pairing enjoyed in Mediterranean Mersin harbor stalls.
